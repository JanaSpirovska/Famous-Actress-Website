Natalie Portman was born in Israel on June 9th, 1981. Her parents are Jewish and have Polish,Russian,Austrian and Romanian roots. Portman's given birth name was Neta-Lee Hershlag; Neta-Lee is a Hebrew name. When she landed a role in "Leon: The Professional" in 1994, she adopted the stage name of Portman. Portman is the maiden name of her grandmother.

 

Education - Harvard University (A Bachelor of Arts in Psychology). A straight-A student since grade school, she graduated with a couple of honours certificates from Syosset High School in June,1999. She spent a semester in Israel studying spoken Arabic, spoken Hebrew, the history of Israel, the history of Islam, and the Anthrolopogy of Violence.

Her favourite subjects were biology,math,science,politics,psychology, and various languages. 

 

Occupations - Actress, director, producer

If she didn't decide to be an actress, she would've been one of the following: doctor, , veterinarian, astronaut, broadway dancer, scientist, photographer.

 

Spouse - Bejamin Millepied (m. 2012; div 2024)

 

Children - Two children, a boy Aleph born on June 7th 2011. And Amalia born on February 22, 2017.

 

Where does she live?  - She lived in Soho NYC for many years before moving to the west coast and Los Angeles. After getting married to Benjamin Millepied, the family moved to Paris,France in 2014. After Benjamin decided to leave the Paris Opera Ballet in 2016, the family moved back to Los Angeles.

Early life

Portman and her family first lived in Washington, D.C., they later moved to Connecticut in 1988 and then to Long Island in the year 1990. Natalie has attended both Charles E. Smith Jewish Day School and the Solomon Schechter Day School of Nassau County. Portman has described herself as ambitious and hard working and felt like she was more serious than the other kids her age. When Portman was just ten years old, she was approached by a Revlon agent at a Pizza restaurant. The agent asked her if she wanted to become a child model but Natalie turned down the offer.

 

Hershlag auditioned for the leading role in Luc Besson's Léon: The Professional (1994). This is when she adopted her grandmother's maiden name, Portman, as her stage name. After filming, Portman went back to school. She enrolled at the Stagedoor Manor performing arts camp, where she portrayed Anne Shirley in a staging of Anne of Green Gables. Michael Mann recognised her talent in The Professional and offered her the role of the stepdaughter of Al Pacino's character in the movie Heat(1995).
