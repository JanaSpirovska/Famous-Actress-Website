Fun facts

Natalie Portman has dual citizenship in both Israel and the United States. She was born in Jerusalem but grew up in Washington, D.C., Connecticut, and Long Island throughout her childhood.

Natalie turned vegetarian when she was just eight years old. She has actively switched between being vegan or vegatarian.

"Team Sleep" is an American experimental alternative rock group. They named a song 'Natalie Portman' but when the band's album was released, they changed the name of the song.

When Natalie was nine years old, she was approached by a beauty scout from Revlon cosmestics who wanted to introduce her to the world of modelling, she declined since she preferred acting.

Natalie co-authored a scientific paper in high school Natalie wrote the paper titled "A Simple Method to Demonstrate the Enzymatic Production of Hydrogen from Sugar." With the help of the scientists Ian Hurley and Jonathan Woodward. The paper was included in the Intel Science Talent Search as well as Harvard's Astrophysics Data System.

Natalie Portman does not have a TV in her home, it is a personal choice. She uses a computer to watch shows or movies instead.

Natalie can speak 6 languages - Portman can speak Hebrew, English, French, German, Japanese and Spanish!
