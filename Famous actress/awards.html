Academy Awards:

  • 2004: Nominated for Best Supporting Actress for Closer.
  • 2010: Won Best Actress for Black Swan.
  • 2016: Nominated for Best Actress for Jackie.

British Academy Film Awards (BAFTA):

  • 2004: Nominated for Best Actress in a Supporting Role for Closer.
  • 2010: Won Best Actress in a Leading Role for Black Swan.
  • 2016: Nominated for Best Actress in a Leading Role for Jackie.

Golden Globe Awards:

  • 1999: Nominated for Best Supporting Actress – Motion Picture for Anywhere but Here.
  • 2004: Won Best Supporting Actress – Motion Picture for Closer.
  • 2010: Won Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Drama for Black Swan.
  • 2016: Nominated for Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Drama for Jackie.
  • 2023: Nominated for Best Actress – Motion Picture Comedy or Musical for May December.

Screen Actors Guild (SAG) Awards:

  • 2010: Won Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Leading Role for Black Swan; also nominated for Outstanding Performance by a Cast in a Motion Picture for the same film.
  • 2016: Nominated for Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Leading Role for Jackie.

Other Notable Awards:

  • 2010: Won the Independent Spirit Award for Best Female Lead for Black Swan.
  • 2016: Won the Critics' Choice Movie Award for Best Actress for Jackie.
  • 2017: Received the Environmental Media Association's Ongoing Commitment Award.

Throughout her career, Portman has amassed a total of 39 wins and 84 nominations across various award bodies.
